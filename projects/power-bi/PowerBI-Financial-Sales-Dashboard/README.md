# Financial Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Microsoft's Financials sample dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, and geographic trends.

**Status:** In Progress — Learning and building step by step.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze total sales and profit.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Track total units sold.
- Understand product-level performance.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power BI Data Visualization
- Financials Sample Dataset

## Dashboard KPIs

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | ~$118M |
| Total Profit | ~$16.89M |
| Units Sold | ~1.13M |
| Unique Products | 6 |

## Visualizations Completed

- Sales by Product — Column Chart
- Sales by Country — Horizontal Bar Chart
- Total Sales — KPI Card
- Total Profit — KPI Card
- Units Sold — KPI Card
- Unique Products — KPI Card

## Initial Findings

- The United States and Canada are among the highest-performing countries by sales.
- Mexico generates the lowest total sales among the five countries in this dataset.
